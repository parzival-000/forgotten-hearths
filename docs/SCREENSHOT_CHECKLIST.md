# Screenshot Checklist

Capture with the default resource pack first. Keep the HUD off unless the status message or Comforted icon is the subject.

- [ ] **Cold arrival:** a three-quarter view of the woodland cottage in light rain or morning fog, with the dark hearth visible through the opening.
- [ ] **Echo under the moss:** the first interaction at the cold hearth, framed close enough to read its soot and green-gray palette.
- [ ] **Warmth returns:** the rekindled hearth at blue hour, with amber light and particles against the cold ruin.
- [ ] **Ordinary traces:** a tabletop or floor-level view showing the kettle, crock, and quilt restoration places.
- [ ] **Before and after pair:** the same camera position before discovery and after full restoration.
- [ ] **A useful refuge:** a player resting beside the completed hearth with the Comforted icon visible.
- [ ] **Three homes:** one clean daylight establishing shot of each woodland, plains, and taiga variant.
- [ ] **Recipe spread:** the three foods, repaired relics, and recipe fragment arranged in a simple survival inventory or on nearby blocks.

Avoid shader-only shots for the main gallery. One optional shader image is useful after the default-lighting set proves that the mod reads clearly on its own.
