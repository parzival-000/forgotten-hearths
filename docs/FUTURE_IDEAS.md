# Future Ideas

These ideas were intentionally left outside the 0.1.0 scope so the core restoration loop could stay reliable and complete.

- A small chimney-clearing or door-repair marker for a fourth homestead layout
- Rare regional material palettes without making each site harder to recognize
- A restrained memory-echo particle vignette when a hearth is first rekindled
- Optional campfire-style cooking at a restored hearth
- Advancement criteria for discovering every homestead variant
- More household relic combinations drawn from ordinary Minecraft materials
- Structure integration tags for carefully tested biome mods
- A data-driven extension point for addon homestead templates

Settlers, village simulation, custom dimensions, large quest systems, and freeform building detection are not planned. They would pull the mod away from its small exploration-and-restoration identity.
